Exoplanet Atmospheres

I study exoplanets of all kinds, as long as they possess an atmosphere. From the perspective of physics and chemistry, the governing principles are universal: atmospheric processes are fundamentally determined by temperature, pressure, chemical composition, photons, and physical transports. Rooted in a rigorous physical chemistry background, I seek to develop a precise, first-principles understanding of exoplanet atmospheric physics and chemistry across a wide range of planetary environments. Ultimately, I hope to apply this understanding to identify potentially Habitable Worlds,, including intriguing environments such as water worlds, and to better understand the conditions that make planets capable of supporting life.
